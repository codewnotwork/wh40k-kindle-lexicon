# Fenrisian (Juvjk) terms. Word list compiled from the Lexicanum "Fenris Vulgaris"
# page, which draws mainly on the Fenrisian Lexicon appendix of The Wolftime.

# ---- A
Adjarr | | (Fenrisian) Lifeblood; the Rune of Blood.
Aelfkid | | (Fenrisian) Wood sprites.
Aett | | (Fenrisian) Clan-home: hearth, fireplace, den. "The Aett" is what the Space Wolves call their fortress-monastery, known to the Imperium as the Fang.
Aettgard | | (Fenrisian) A leader's chosen warriors; another name for the Wolf Guard.
Aettjarl | | (Fenrisian) The leader of a settlement.
Aett-rune | aettrune | (Fenrisian) Home rune: the sigil of a Great Company.
Aett-skjald | aettskjald | (Fenrisian) The lorekeeper of a settlement.
Aett-vater | aettvater | (Fenrisian) Home-father: a commander or captain.
Alfathir | Allfather, Alfather | (Fenrisian) The Allfather: the Emperor of Mankind.
Almáttigrbíta | Almattigrbita | (Fenrisian) "The hunter's cut", likely meaning a killing blow. Leman Russ favoured the word; the Chogorian (Khorchin) equivalent is Berkutra.

# ---- B
Balka | | (Fenrisian, vulgar) Testicles; "bollocks".
Baresark | baresarks | (Fenrisian) Berserker.
Bludhaer | | (Fenrisian) Hour of Blood: battle or war.
Bludhalle | | (Fenrisian) Blood hall: the medicae or apothecarion, home of the Fleshmakers (Wolf Priests).

# ---- D
Damget | | (Fenrisian) "Children of Damnation!" A curse or insult.
Drekkar | | (Fenrisian) A type of Fenrisian longship.

# ---- E
Einherjar | | (Fenrisian) "Blood-sworn."
Ekka | | (Fenrisian) Fenrisian pine trees.
Eldkringla | | (Fenrisian) The Wheel of Fire: a stellar region of unstable nebulae and wandering stars, once overrun by greenskins.

# ---- F
Felhird | | (Fenrisian) Beastmen.
Fengr | | (Fenrisian) The wolf within: the bestial nature inside every Space Wolf.
Fenryka | | (Fenrisian) "Of Fenris." See Vlka Fenryka.
Fenrys | | (Fenrisian) Fenris.
Fenrys Hjammar Koldt | | (Fenrisian) "Fenris' hammering cold!" A battle cry.
Fenrys Hjolda | | (Fenrisian) "Fenris forever!" A battle cry.
Fja vø | Fja vo | (Fenrisian) "Go now."
Fjorulalli | | (Fenrisian) The Seal-mother.
Fyrbrod | | (Fenrisian) "Fire-rod": a gun.
Fyr-ent | fyrent | (Fenrisian) A fire giant.
Fyrkaf | | (Fenrisian) Firedrink: a restorative drink.
Fyrmirdra | | (Fenrisian) Fire serpent: a wyrm or dragon.
Fyrstrom | | (Fenrisian) Firestorm: an explosion.

# ---- G
Gann | | (Fenrisian) Death; the Rune of Death.
Geldfut | | (Fenrisian) An outstanding task or purpose: a mission.
Geldwhal | | (Fenrisian) A species of Fenrisian whale.
Gmorl | | (Fenrisian) Fate; the Rune of Fate.
Gothi | | (Fenrisian) A witch, shaman or Rune Priest.
Gungnir | | (Fenrisian) Wolfsbane.

# ---- H
Hamarrki | | (Fenrisian) Spine.
Hamarrkiskaldi | | (Fenrisian) "Spine story": instinct, gut feeling.
Hearthegn | | (Fenrisian) Champion; specifically the Great Wolf's Champion.
Hel | | (Fenrisian) Damnation: a place beneath the ice for those who die badly.
Hersir | | (Fenrisian) A military leader's rank.
Hja | | (Fenrisian) Yes.
Hjammar | | (Fenrisian) Hammer.
Hjolda | | (Fenrisian) An exclamation of surprise, welcome or frustration.
Hnefatafl | | (Fenrisian) A strategy board game popular on Fenris.
Holmgang | holmgangs | (Fenrisian) An honour duel between heroes.
Hould | | (Fenrisian) The Hold.
Husjarl | | (Fenrisian) A trusted advisor or second-in-command; a lieutenant.
Huskaerl | Huscarl, Huscarls, Huskaerls | (Fenrisian) A soldier or armed servant. In Old Fenrisian, also the Wolf Guard.

# ---- J
Ja hjøld | Ja hjold | (Fenrisian) An expression of approval.
Jarl | jarls | (Fenrisian) A lord; among the Space Wolves, a Wolf Lord.
Jarl-deck | jarldeck | (Fenrisian) A ship's command deck or strategium.
Jarlhalle | | (Fenrisian) A lord's hall.
Jarlship | jarlships | (Fenrisian) A medium warship, roughly a light cruiser.
Jøva | Jova | (Fenrisian) "Look!" or "Behold!"
Juvi | | (Fenrisian) "The Word": a spokesperson; truth.
Juvjk | Juvyk | (Fenrisian) The hearth-cant: Fenrisian as spoken off the battlefield. Compare Wurgen, the battle-cant.
Juvykka | | (Fenrisian) The Fenrisian language (Fenris Vulgaris), the dominant dialect of Low Gothic on Fenris.

# ---- K
Kaerl | kaerls | (Fenrisian) A mortal servant or thrall of the Space Wolves.
Karlship | karlships | (Fenrisian) A larger-than-usual longship; a cruiser.
Kjalhalla | | (Fenrisian) Apparently the afterlife.
Koenigsgard | | (Fenrisian) King's Guard: the Great Wolf's retinue.
Koldt | | (Fenrisian) Cold.
Konungur | | (Fenrisian) King.

# ---- M
Maleficarum | | (Fenrisian usage) Bad magic; also bad luck, an ill omen or an evil spirit.
Mjød | Mjod | (Fenrisian) Mead-like Fenrisian drink, potent enough to affect a Space Marine.
Móðir | Modir, Mothir | (Fenrisian) Mother.
Morkai | | (Fenrisian) The Deathwolf: a two-headed wolf of Fenrisian legend that guards the gates of death.

# ---- O
Örlendr | Orlendr | (Fenrisian) Outsider, alien; can also mean intruder.
Ótrúin | Otruin | (Fenrisian) Betrayers, faithless, kinslayers: traitors, especially of the Traitor Legions.
Oververse | | (Fenrisian) The afterlife.

# ---- R
Ragnarok | | (Fenrisian) Ending; the Rune of Ending.
Riven | rivens | (Fenrisian) A unit of 500 kaerls.
Rivenmaster | rivenmasters | (Fenrisian) A kaerl captain of a military unit.
Runejarl | runejarls, Rune Jarl | (Fenrisian) A Rune Priest: a Space Wolves Librarian (psyker).
Runekast | runekasts | (Fenrisian) A sorcerer: an untrustworthy user of wyrd.

# ---- T

# ---- U

# ---- V
Vaerangi | vaerengr, varagyr | (Fenrisian) The Wolf Guard.
Vahk | Vahk meh | (Fenrisian) An exclamation of surprise.
Valkjyr | | (Fenrisian) Angels: the choosers of the slain in battle.
Varg-Ulf | vargulf, varg-ulfs | (Fenrisian) A wolf driven mad by wyrd.
Vlka | | (Fenrisian) Wolves.
Vlka Fenryka | | (Fenrisian) "Wolves of Fenris": the Space Wolves' own name for their Legion and Chapter.
Volda | | (Fenrisian) The world.
Volda Hammarki | | (Fenrisian) A daemon: a corrupt wight, Neverborn, "non-thing".

# ---- W
War-aett | waraett | (Fenrisian) A base or command centre.
War-skelt | warskelt | (Fenrisian) A battle hymn.
Weregost | weregosts | (Fenrisian) A talisman or likeness. Each Great Company's symbol is its weregost.
Wight | wights | (Fenrisian usage) A corpse-ghost.
Wulfhalle | | (Fenrisian) Wolf Hall: the Great Wolf's personal chambers.
Wurgen | | (Fenrisian) The battle-cant: the clipped Fenrisian the Space Wolves speak while fighting. Compare Juvjk, the hearth-cant.
Wyrd | | (Fenrisian) Fate; also magic, or the warp. Used as a prefix: wyrdflesh, wyrdfulk, wyrdstorm, etc.
Wyrd-marked | wyrdmarked | (Fenrisian) Bearing an ill-omened fate; born under a bad star.
Wyrdfast | | (Fenrisian) "Fate-caught."
Wyrding blade | wyrding blades | (Fenrisian) A bladed psychic weapon steeped in the power of the Underverse.
Wyrm | wyrms | (Fenrisian) A dragon.

# ---- Y
Ygdras | | (Fenrisian) A tree-giant.
Ymir | | (Fenrisian) Earth; the Rune of Earth.
